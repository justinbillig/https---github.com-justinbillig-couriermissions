# Courier Missions Brand Voice

Version 1.0 · November 2, 2026

All website copy follows this guide. It sits alongside [BUSINESS-SPEC.md](./BUSINESS-SPEC.md): the spec says what each page covers and which facts it uses; this file says how the words should sound. Facts and numbers still come only from the spec, the team, or Courier Missions' own posts.

---

## 1. Sound like our Facebook posts, not the grant application

Much of the first-draft copy came from the grant application and the mission statement, which were written for funders. Our Facebook posts already have the right voice: warm, specific, and faith-forward.

> "When the rains don't come, you find another way to bring life to the land."
>
> "There was a time when rain fell inside this church…"
>
> "From unfinished walls to a beacon of hope."
>
> "Let that sink in… 141 people will now hold truth, hope, and the Gospel in their hands."

## 2. The voice: a blend of three modes

**Decided:** field notes are the base voice; use a letter from friends when asking people to act, and praise report for celebrations.

| Mode | What it sounds like | Use it for | Sample line (tone only) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Field notes** (base voice) | Concrete and scene-first. Opens with a moment, a place or a number. Short sentences. Shows rather than explains. | Our Work, ministry pages, church projects, Harvest of Hope, Impact | "Monday, the roof went up on Imarira Church. By Friday, the congregation was worshiping under it." |
| **A letter from friends** | Warm and personal, as if Heather and Corey were talking over coffee. Uses "we" and "you"; gives the donor real credit. | About, Get Involved, donate prompts and CTA bands, Contact | "We didn't set out to build churches. Then we stood in one where the rain came through the roof." |
| **Praise report** | Faith-forward and joyful. God gets the glory; the gift is part of the story. | Completed churches, dedications, milestones, the annual Impact review | "What some thought might take years, God did in months. Mayungu Church is finished, and 600 people ate together to celebrate." |

The sample lines show tone only. Real copy uses only confirmed facts.

## 3. Voice rules

1. **Lead with a person or a moment, not the organization.** Not "Courier Missions provides…" but "Every school day, 100 students at the Deaf school sit down to a hot meal."
2. **Turn numbers into people.** "375 students fed" is fine on a stats bar. In a paragraph: "275 kids at Shiongo who used to sit through afternoon lessons hungry."
3. **Name things.** Wetai, Mayungu, Mark, the Deaf school, not "communities" or "local partners."
4. **Local leaders are the heroes; donors are partners.** Courier Missions comes alongside. No savior framing, no pity.
5. **Plain words.** Cut grant language (see the word list).
6. **Faith spoken naturally, and meant.** "We pray with people," not "spiritual development programming."
7. **Short sentences, but vary them.** A short punch line after a longer sentence is our signature move: "So we dug a well."
8. **Only real details.** Every scene, number, name and quote comes from the team, the spec, or Courier Missions' own posts. If a sentence needs a detail we don't have, write it without the detail.
9. **Talk to one reader.** "You" and "your gift," not "donors" or "supporters" in the third person.
10. **Children are never the subject of a story they didn't tell.** No names, no identifying details (see BUSINESS-SPEC.md 6.4).

## 4. Word list

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| beneficiaries, recipients | kids, families, students, the congregation, people (or name them) |
| vulnerable populations, underserved communities | the name of the place or the people |
| holistic | (cut it, or say what: food, faith and friendship) |
| empower | help, train, equip, come alongside, pay a fair wage |
| initiatives, programming | the work, the farm, the clinic, lunch at school |
| sustainable development | it keeps going, it pays for itself, it lasts |
| leverage, maximize, facilitate | use, grow, help |
| spiritual development | faith, prayer, discipleship, Bibles |
| stakeholders, partners (alone) | the pastor at Wetai, Mark, our team in Kenya |

Keep: hope, purpose, gift, together, pray, deliver, serve. These are the brand's own words ("Delivering Hope, Serving with Purpose").

## 5. Before and after

| Now | In our voice |
|---|---|
| "Courier Missions is a U.S.-based 501(c)(3) Christian nonprofit serving vulnerable communities through humanitarian aid, education, sustainable development and community partnership." | "We're a small, volunteer-led ministry from Indiana. We work alongside pastors and leaders in Kenya, Sierra Leone and here at home, feeding kids, building churches and showing up when disaster hits." |
| "We provide school meals for 375 children: 100 students at the Kakamega School for the Deaf and 275 at Shiongo Primary School." | "At Shiongo Primary, lunch used to be a maybe. Now 275 kids know they'll eat, and so do 100 students at the Kakamega School for the Deaf." |
| "Quarterly outreach clinics offer consultations, medicines, screenings and health education." | "Four times a year, the line forms before sunrise. Moms with babies, grandparents, kids with fevers. Our clinics see everyone who comes." **(Not confirmed: don't use until the team confirms these details.)** |
| "Instead of only buying food, we grow it." | "Buying food for hundreds of kids every day adds up fast. So we started growing it." |

## 6. Where each part of the site sits

| Part of the site | Mode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Home hero and lead | Field notes | One clear, concrete sentence about what we do. |
| Stats rows | Plain numbers | Short labels; no adjectives. |
| Ministry area pages and church projects | Field notes | Open each section with a scene or a fact from the posts. |
| Completed church or project announcements | Praise report | Celebrate, credit God and the givers, name the church. |
| About and leadership | A letter from friends | Warm, first person plural. |
| Donate buttons, CTA bands, Get Involved | A letter from friends | Speak to "you"; say what the gift does. |
| Impact review | Praise report over field notes | Joyful, but every claim backed by a number from the review. |
| Forms, errors, 404 | Plain and friendly | Short, clear instructions; a touch of warmth is fine ("This page has gone on a mission."). |

## 7. Checklist before publishing any copy

- [ ] Does it open with a person, a place or a moment?
- [ ] Is every fact, number and name confirmed (spec, team, or our own post)?
- [ ] Are grant words gone (word list)?
- [ ] Is the reader spoken to as "you"?
- [ ] Are local leaders and congregations the main characters?
- [ ] Is there at least one short sentence that lands the point?
